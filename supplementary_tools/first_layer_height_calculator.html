# Δs (m) Growth Rate Layers Total (m) Total (mm) Last Layer (m) Expansion Ratio

Total Height = Δs × (rn − 1) / (r − 1), where Δs is the first layer height, r is the growth rate, and n is the number of layers. When r = 1, Total Height = Δs × n. The last layer height is Δs × rn−1, and the expansion ratio is the last layer divided by the first.
